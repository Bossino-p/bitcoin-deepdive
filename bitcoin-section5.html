Truth 01

Predictable Issuance

Every bitcoin that will ever exist follows a schedule written in 2008. On any given day you can calculate exactly how many bitcoin exist, how many will be mined tomorrow, and the precise block at which the last satoshi will be created. No central bank in history has offered this.

Truth 02

Asymptotic Scarcity

The supply curve approaches 21 million but never reaches it — the halving means each epoch issues half the previous. This is not a marketing claim. It is the mathematical property of a geometric series. 94% of all bitcoin will have been mined by 2024. The remaining 6% takes over 100 years.

Truth 03

The Fee Transition

As the block subsidy decays toward zero, miner revenue must come increasingly from transaction fees. This is Bitcoin's one open economic question — whether fee markets will provide sufficient security budget at scale. It is not a flaw. It is the honest design tension.

Every fiat currency ever created has trended toward zero purchasing power. Bitcoin is the first monetary asset with a mathematically enforced ceiling. Whether that makes it sound money or a speculative asset depends entirely on whether you trust the code or the committee.
